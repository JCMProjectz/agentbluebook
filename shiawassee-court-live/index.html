What you're watching

The 35th Circuit Court is Shiawassee County's main trial court. It handles felony criminal cases, civil lawsuits over $25,000, divorce and custody, and appeals from district court.

Arraignment
The defendant hears the charges and enters a plea.
Pretrial
The lawyers and judge check where the case stands. Plea agreements are often worked out here.
Plea
The defendant pleads guilty or no contest, often under an agreement with the prosecutor.
Sentencing
After a conviction, the judge decides the punishment.
Motion hearing
The judge rules on a request from one side, such as dismissing a charge.
Probation violation
The court decides whether someone broke the terms of their probation.
