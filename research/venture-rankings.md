# Venture Rankings — Next Project After Michigan LLC Service

**Date:** September 2026
**Filter applied:** high traffic potential, high conversion intent, MRR-first, ≥90% end-to-end automatable, high ceiling (national, not state-capped), and maximum reuse of the Michigan LLC Service pipeline (intent-driven SEO funnel → self-serve checkout → n8n/Claude fulfillment → email delivery).

**Context:** Michigan LLC Service produced its first 2 paying customers in one week, ~2 months post-launch, with zero sales effort. That validates the pattern: *transactional compliance products bought on intent, not relationships*. Everything below is ranked by how hard it leans into that pattern.

---

## The Rankings

### 🥇 1. MarkSentry — Automated Trademark Monitoring ($89–99/yr) — **DEPLOY NOW**
- **The gap:** monitoring runs $200–600/yr/mark industry-wide; attorney-managed is $500–1,500/yr. The "budget" players still charge $169/yr (Markify US) or $39.99/mo (L4SB). The underlying USPTO data is **free**. A clean $89/yr fully-automated watch is priced under everyone while holding ~95% gross margin.
- **Why it wins every filter:**
  - **National from day one** — not state-capped like LLC filings. Every one of the ~5.5M new US businesses/yr is a prospect. Highest ceiling on this list.
  - **Pure software** — USPTO scan → Claude conflict analysis → email report. No filing on behalf of anyone, no unauthorized-practice-of-law exposure (monitoring ≠ legal advice, with disclaimers). 100% automatable, literally zero human minutes per customer per month.
  - **MRR by nature** — protection products renew silently; churn = business closure, same dynamics that make registered agents gold.
  - **Traffic engine built-in** — a FREE "trademark conflict check" tool captures massive "trademark search" / "is my business name taken" query volume, then upsells monitoring. Same free-tool→paid funnel as LLC formation.
  - **Perfect cross-sell** — every Michigan LLC customer just chose a name they haven't protected. One email at day 7: "3 similar marks were filed last quarter — watch yours for $89/yr." As you expand to 5 more states, this product scales with the formation funnel automatically.
- **Risks:** USPTO search API access needs a key/provider choice (solved, see scaffold README); "trademark" SERPs have national competitors (mitigate via long-tail + owned funnel cross-sell).

### 🥈 2. Annual Report Autopilot, 50 states (the expansion you already planned — accelerate it)
- Not a new project; it's the LLC service's compounding layer. Michigan Annual Statement ($25 state fee + $40–50 service) → replicate per state. Every formation customer converts to a compliance subscriber.
- **Key point:** ship the *compliance calendar free tier* nationally **before** the formation service reaches those states — it captures "[state] annual report due date" searches now and pre-builds the email list your top-5-state launch will convert.
- Ranked #2 only because it's already yours; the marginal decision is sequencing, not building.

### 🥉 3. LLC Dissolution Service ("close my LLC")
- **The gap:** everyone fights over formation; almost nobody productizes closing. "How to dissolve an LLC in [state]" is high-intent, low-competition search. ~20% of businesses close within 2 years — your own churned formation customers are the funnel.
- One-time $99–149 + state fee; fully automatable (certificate of dissolution + final-compliance checklist generated by Claude). Same pipeline, same state expansion map. Weak on MRR (one-shot), which is why it's #3 — but conversion intent is the highest on this list and it monetizes churn that's otherwise worth $0.

### 4. License & CE Renewal Autopilot for licensed professionals
- Track license renewals + continuing-education deadlines for real estate agents, contractors, nurses, cosmetologists. $5–9/mo. Starts with **Michigan real estate agents — direct AgentBluebook audience synergy** (you already own their attention and affiliate rails).
- Big ceiling (tens of millions of licensed pros), pure-software reminders. Ranked #4 because low price point means volume dependence, and per-state/per-board data ingestion is grindy (though Claude can maintain the deadline database).

### 5. Free "Name Check Everything" tool (traffic weapon, not a product)
- One input → LLC name availability (state DB) + domain + trademark + social handles. Feeds leads to LLC formation *and* MarkSentry simultaneously. Build it as the shared top-of-funnel for the whole portfolio rather than a standalone venture.

### 6. Nonprofit / 501(c)(3) formation
- Higher ticket ($400–600 service fee), less crowded than LLC. But IRS Form 1023 involvement is only ~70% automatable and error costs are high. Do later, once the multi-state engine is boring.

### 7. Property tax appeal automation (outside-the-box, parked)
- High value per win, comps are data-automatable, contingency pricing. But seasonal, county-by-county process sprawl, and semi-manual evidence filing. Revisit in 2027.

### 8. Azee-style local agency competitor — **shelved**
- Full analysis in `research/azee-competitive-analysis.md`. Delivery automates; the *business* (sales, approvals, relationships) doesn't. Fails the passivity filter. Salvage: sell the automated website factory as a self-serve add-on at LLC checkout ("Business-in-a-Box") — no agency posture.
