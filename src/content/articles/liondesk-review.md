## What Is LionDesk?

LionDesk is a real estate CRM built specifically for solo agents and small teams who need automated follow-up without paying enterprise prices. At $25/month for the starter plan, it targets agents who know they need a CRM but aren't ready to commit to Follow Up Boss at $69/month or kvCORE at $499/month.

The platform launched in 2014 and has been refined specifically for real estate workflows — lead capture, drip campaigns, text and video messaging, and pipeline tracking.

| Pro | $49/mo | 1 | + Video texting + bulk texting |

## What LionDesk Does Well

**Video texting is a genuine differentiator.** LionDesk's Pro plan lets you send video messages directly in text threads. Agents who use this report dramatically higher response rates from cold leads — a quick 30-second video from a real face converts better than any text template.

**Drip campaigns are plug-and-play.** Unlike Follow Up Boss where you build sequences from scratch, LionDesk ships with pre-built drip campaigns for buyers, sellers, past clients, and cold leads. For agents who don't have time to architect automation, this removes the biggest setup barrier.

**The price is genuinely hard to beat.** At $25/month, LionDesk is the most affordable full-featured real estate CRM on the market. Competing tools at this price point — HubSpot free tier, basic Zoho — lack real estate-specific workflows. LionDesk was built for agents.

## What LionDesk Gets Wrong

**The UI is showing its age.** The interface feels like 2018. Navigation is cluttered, mobile experience is inconsistent, and new users report a steeper learning curve than competitors with modern UI like Follow Up Boss or Sierra Interactive.

**Lead source integrations top out at 100.** Follow Up Boss connects to 250+ sources. For agents running multiple paid lead sources alongside organic channels, LionDesk's integration library has gaps — particularly with newer lead gen platforms.

**Team features are an afterthought.** The Pro+ plan at $99/month technically supports up to 5 users, but lead distribution and team accountability features feel bolted on rather than built in. The moment you hire your first buyer's agent, you'll feel the platform straining.

## Who Should Buy LionDesk

**Buy it if:** You're a solo agent with one or two lead sources, under 100 leads per month, and you want a real estate-specific CRM without paying Follow Up Boss prices. The pre-built drip campaigns mean you're up and running in an afternoon.

**Skip it if:** You manage a team, run three or more lead sources, or prioritize a modern UI. At that stage, Follow Up Boss at $69/month is worth every extra dollar.

**The payoff scenario:** A solo agent spending $25/month on LionDesk who converts just one extra lead per quarter from better follow-up automation earns back 12 months of subscription costs from a single transaction. The ROI math is obvious.

**The waste scenario:** A two-person team paying $99/month for LionDesk Pro+ and fighting the platform's weak lead distribution every day. That frustration costs more in lost deals than the $200/month difference to Follow Up Boss Pro.
