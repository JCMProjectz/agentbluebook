## What Is Follow Up Boss?

Follow Up Boss is a real estate CRM built around one core problem: agents who get leads from multiple sources — Zillow, Realtor.com, their website, Facebook ads — and lose half of them because follow-up falls through the cracks.

It solves that by pulling every lead into one place, auto-routing them to the right agent on your team, and building the follow-up sequence for you. The platform integrates with 250+ lead sources, which is where its reputation was built.

## What Follow Up Boss Does Well

**Lead aggregation is genuinely excellent.** The platform pulls leads from over 250 sources without manual setup. If you're running Zillow Premier Agent, Facebook Lead Ads, and your IDX website at the same time, Follow Up Boss catches everything. This alone prevents the single biggest money leak in real estate: leads going cold because nobody followed up.

**Smart Lists work the way your brain works.** Instead of managing a static list, Smart Lists auto-update based on behavior. "New leads from the last 24 hours" or "contacts I haven't touched in a week" appear automatically — so your daily to-do list builds itself.

**Team accountability is built into the product.** Team leaders can see exactly what every agent is doing, track response times, and identify which leads aren't getting enough attention. For growing teams, this replaces the need for a sales manager watching CRM activity manually.

## What Follow Up Boss Gets Wrong

**It's expensive for what solo agents actually need.** At $69/month for one user, you're paying for infrastructure designed for teams. If you're a solo agent with one lead source (say, just your sphere and referrals), you're overpaying significantly compared to LionDesk at $25/month or CINC's bundled model.

**No built-in transaction management.** Once a lead converts to a client, Follow Up Boss stops being useful. You'll need a separate tool — Dotloop, Skyslope, or similar — which adds cost and friction. Competing platforms like kvCORE try to cover this natively.

**Pricing jumps are steep.** Going from the $69 solo plan to the $299 team plan when you add your first team member is a painful $230/month increase. There's no middle tier.

## Who Should Buy Follow Up Boss

**Buy it if:** You're running leads from three or more sources and leads are slipping through the cracks. You manage a team and need visibility into agent activity. You're spending $1,000+/month on lead generation and losing ROI to poor follow-up.

**Skip it if:** You're a solo agent with one lead source, a tight budget, or under 50 leads per month. At that volume, LionDesk ($25/mo) or even a well-configured free CRM covers everything you need at a fraction of the cost.

**The payoff scenario:** One agent told us that switching to Follow Up Boss cut their lead response time from 4 hours to 8 minutes on average. At a 21x lead conversion advantage for sub-5-minute responses (documented by MIT), that one improvement can pay for years of subscription costs from a single deal.

**The waste scenario:** A solo agent with 20 leads per month and a single lead source paying $69/month is spending $828/year for automated routing they don't need. LionDesk does the same job for $300/year.

## Our Verdict

Follow Up Boss is the best real estate CRM on the market for teams and agents running multiple lead sources. At $69/month for solo agents or $299/month for teams, the ROI is clear if you're losing leads to poor follow-up.

If you're a solo agent with a single lead source and under 50 leads per month, it's overkill. Start with LionDesk and upgrade when you outgrow it.
