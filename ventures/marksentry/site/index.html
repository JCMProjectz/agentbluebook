Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. MarkSentry is an information and monitoring service, not a law firm, and nothing we send is legal advice. When an alert looks serious, we'll say so and suggest you speak with a trademark attorney — many offer free consultations.

I haven't registered a trademark. Is this still useful?

Especially then. If someone else registers a similar mark first, they can force you to rebrand — signs, domain, packaging, everything. Monitoring tells you the moment that risk appears so you can respond, or file your own application first.

What exactly do you scan?

New trademark applications published in USPTO public records, compared weekly against your watch profile. Alerts include the filing details, similarity assessment, and links to the official USPTO record.

What happens when a conflict is found?

You get an email within days of the filing becoming public: what was filed, who filed it, how similar it is, and your realistic options — from "ignore it" to "talk to an attorney about opposing before the deadline."

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in one click from any email we send. Your monitoring runs through the end of the period you paid for.
